<objective>
Build the execute_steps call-site scanner: a pure `scanExecuteSteps(content)` function plus a cached `parseExecuteStepsFileContent` wrapper, modeled on the sibling `stepsParser.ts` / `featureParser.ts` conventions. This is the foundation the mapping funnel (Plan 02) and parse-cycle wiring (Plan 03) build on.

Purpose: Discover `context.execute_steps("...")` embedded steps in `.py` files as structured records so reference counting can later union them into step mappings (REFS-01/02/03). The scanner's zero-false-positive "skip silently on anything that isn't a recognizable literal" policy is the design constraint that makes the whole feature safe.

Output: `executeStepsParser.ts` (new module with the scanner, cache, and lookup helpers), a net-new `executeStepsKeywordRe` export in `gherkinPatterns.ts`, and a unit suite covering the scanner edge-case checklist.
</objective>
